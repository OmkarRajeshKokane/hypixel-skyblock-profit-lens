Crafting → Bazaar guide

Price the full recipe before crafting

Crafting profit is not the completed item price by itself. This page totals every required ingredient at the chosen source, calculates how many ingredients make each crafted item, then compares the completed item with the after-tax Bazaar Sell Instantly payout. A recipe is only profitable when that payout is greater than the complete ingredient cost.

Some SkyBlock items have more than one enchantment tier. The calculator follows the listed recipe step rather than assuming that a base material always crafts directly into the final enchanted item. For example, a route may first make Enchanted Melon Slices before it can make an Enchanted Melon. Read the displayed recipe and ingredient quantities before buying anything.

When the calculator offers NPC and Bazaar ingredient sources, compare both. NPC purchases can have daily limits; Bazaar purchases can change in price and depth. The maximum plan uses your available coins and inventory space, but crafting can change stack usage, so leave space for ingredients and results.

Crafting checklist

  • Verify the exact recipe tier and output item.
  • Multiply each ingredient unit price by its required quantity.
  • Apply Bazaar tax only to the completed item’s instant sale.
  • Check the crafting screen and Bazaar confirmation before committing coins.
