Privacy Policy

This calculator requests public Hypixel market data directly from Hypixel to display profit estimates. It stores only calculator settings in your browser's local storage.

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Review the advertising provider's privacy policy for details and available controls.

Public visit counter

The calculator footer shows an approximate total page-view counter supplied by Hits. It counts page loads rather than unique people and is delivered as a third-party image request. This site does not run its own visitor-profile database.
