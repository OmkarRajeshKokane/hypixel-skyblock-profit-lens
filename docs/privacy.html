Last updated: 16 October 2026. This policy covers the SkyBlock Profit Lens calculator and information pages in this project.

Information the calculator uses

You do not need a player account, password, API key, or payment details to use this tool. Coin balances and inventory settings are values you enter for calculations; the tool does not read your Minecraft account.

Storage on your device

The calculator uses local storage to remember your coin budget, inventory slots, sale-tax setting, and NPC purchase-limit marks. These settings are not intentionally sent to our server. NPC marks remain until you reset them; they do not automatically expire at the game's daily reset. Use “Reset NPC daily marks” to clear marks, or clear this site's browser data to remove all saved settings. If storage is unavailable, calculations still work but settings may not survive a reload.

Hosting and external requests

GitHub Pages delivers this site. Your browser contacts Hypixel for public item and market data and Hits for the page-view counter where displayed. Those providers receive technical request information, such as your IP address, and may process it under their own policies. A page-view total is not a count of unique people. See GitHub's privacy statement, Hypixel's privacy policy, and Hits for provider information.

Google advertising

Calculator pages include Google AdSense code. Third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies to select ads based on visits to this site and other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize ads using that browsing activity. Ad availability depends on approval, configuration, and applicable consent choices.

To manage or opt out of Google's personalized advertising, use Google Ads Settings. Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's privacy policy. Other vendors or ad networks may also use cookies when their ads are served. You can manage participating third-party providers' personalized-ad choices at YourAdChoices. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily remove all advertising.

Consent choices

Where a consent message is presented, use its controls to choose whether data may be used for advertising and to review the listed advertising partners. Browser cookie controls and clearing site data provide additional controls, but clearing data may also reset saved choices and calculator settings.

Questions and corrections

Report general issues through the public project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not post personal data, credentials, identity documents, or financial details. GitHub processes information you submit there under its own policy.
