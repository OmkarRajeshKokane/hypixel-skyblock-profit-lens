These examples use invented prices to explain the calculator. They are not current market quotes or promises of profit. Check each price and recipe in game before making a trade.

Example 1: Bazaar purchase, NPC sale

Suppose an item costs 10 coins to buy instantly, an NPC pays 12 coins, and 500 items are available at that Bazaar price. With a 1,000-coin budget and two 64-item inventory slots, the limits are 100 affordable items, 128 inventory spaces, and 500 market items. The calculator chooses the smallest: 100 items.

Your purchase costs 1,000 coins. The NPC payout is 1,200 coins. Estimated profit is 200 coins. No Bazaar sale tax is applied because the sale is to an NPC. If only 40 items remain at that quote, the displayed run falls to 40 items and 80 coins of profit. The tool does not assume the next price level is equally cheap.

Example 2: NPC purchase, Bazaar sale

Suppose a shop sells an item for 5 coins and the Bazaar instant-sale quote is 8 coins. At a chosen 1.25% sale tax, net payout is 8 × 0.9875 = 7.9 coins per item. The margin is 2.9 coins.

With 10,000 coins, ten slots, 300 items of demand at the quote, and an unused 640-item NPC allowance, the limits are 2,000, 640, 300, and 640 items. Demand limits the run to 300 items: cost 1,500, net payout 2,370, estimated profit 870 coins. Mark an allowance as used only when appropriate; the checkbox is an all-or-nothing exclusion, not a record of partial purchases.

Example 3: a single crafting step

Assume a supported recipe needs 160 units of one ingredient at 3 coins each. One craft costs 480 coins. If the output sells instantly for 600 coins, a 1.25% tax leaves 592.5 coins: profit is 112.5 coins per craft.

With 1,000 coins, five inventory slots, 400 ingredients available at the quote, and demand for ten outputs, you can fund two crafts, hold ingredients for two, and buy ingredients for two. The result is two crafts, costing 960 coins with 225 coins estimated profit. This is a one-step calculation, not a search over every intermediate recipe.

Why an empty result can be correct

Buy Instantly and Sell Instantly are different sides of the market. An instant purchase for 100 followed by an instant sale for 99 loses money even before tax. The calculator filters out non-positive margins, so “No profitable opportunities” does not mean the data failed to load. Check the status message to distinguish a successful refresh from a network error.

A repeatable check before trading

  1. Refresh and check whether the status reports success.
  2. Enter the coins and slots actually available. Verify your tax setting in game.
  3. Compare the same price direction and quantity in the in-game confirmation.
  4. Check recipe unlocks, NPC eligibility, and remaining limits yourself.
  5. Start with a small transaction when prices are moving. Each row is an alternative use of your budget; do not add all row profits together.
