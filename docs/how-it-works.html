This tool compares public market snapshots for manual in-game decisions. It cannot access your account or execute trades.

Inputs and data sources

Public Bazaar quotes and item metadata come from Hypixel's API. The project stores separate NPC purchase-price and recipe reference files. Those reference values need maintenance after game changes; a live market refresh does not verify every NPC price or recipe.

Net profit

Profit per unit is destination payout minus source cost. For a Bazaar sale, net payout equals the instant-sale quote multiplied by (1 − selected tax / 100). NPC sales do not receive that Bazaar tax deduction.

Quantity limits

The calculation takes the smallest whole quantity allowed by the entered budget, inventory, market quantity at the selected top quote, and supported NPC allowance. Inventory assumes 64 items per slot. This simplified model does not account for sacks, unusual stack sizes, existing inventory, or successive trips. Each row uses the budget independently.

NPC allowance marks

Supported NPC purchases use a 640-item cap. A checked item is treated as unavailable. Partial usage is not tracked, marks are shared between applicable routes in this browser, and they do not expire automatically. Reset them manually when the in-game allowance resets. Check actual NPC rules before buying.

Crafting

Each listed recipe is one conversion step with one ingredient type: ingredient price × recipe quantity is the craft cost. Output demand and available ingredients limit the quantity. The calculator does not verify collection unlocks or optimize a multi-step crafting chain.

Freshness and failures

A successful refresh means requests returned usable data, not that prices are guaranteed until you trade. On a failed refresh the tool clears actionable results and offers another refresh. No profitable rows can also be a valid outcome; read the status message.

See the worked examples for arithmetic you can check by hand. Report incorrect reference data through the issue tracker with public, non-sensitive evidence.
