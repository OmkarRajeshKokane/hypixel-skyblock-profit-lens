NPC → Bazaar guide

Buy from an NPC, then sell instantly at the Bazaar

This route compares an NPC shop purchase with the money received from an instant Bazaar sale. The profit per unit is the after-tax Sell Instantly payout minus the NPC buy price. Because the final sale is on the Bazaar, the selected Bazaar tax is deducted before a row is considered profitable.

NPC shop purchases are restricted by availability. For the items covered by the calculator, the daily purchase limit is 640 items per item and NPC. The daily-limit checkbox is a personal reminder only; it does not communicate with Hypixel or verify your in-game purchases. Mark an item when you have used its allowance so you do not accidentally plan the same purchase twice.

The suggested maximum quantity respects coins, inventory slots, and the listed shop limit. It cannot guarantee an NPC has the item in every situation, nor can it guarantee the Bazaar price when you arrive. Confirm both the shop price and the instant-sell confirmation in game.

Safe workflow

  • Buy only the quantity that fits your remaining NPC limit and inventory.
  • Recheck the Bazaar payout after travelling to the NPC.
  • Use the calculator as research; it cannot place trades for a player.
