Bazaar → NPC guide

When a Bazaar purchase can be sold to an NPC

This route starts with a Buy Instantly purchase on the Bazaar and finishes at an in-game NPC. The useful comparison is simple: NPC sell value minus the Bazaar Buy Instantly price. A positive number means the listed unit has a theoretical margin before you spend time travelling, buying, and selling. Bazaar tax is not deducted here because the final sale happens to an NPC, not on the Bazaar.

Use the available-coins and inventory-slot settings as hard limits. The calculator finds the maximum whole quantity you can afford while fitting it into your inventory, then multiplies the per-unit margin by that quantity. Check the in-game Bazaar confirmation before you buy: Buy Instantly prices can move quickly, and a profitable-looking row can disappear after other players fill orders.

NPC prices are more stable than Bazaar prices, but they are not a promise that every item can be sold to every NPC. Treat this page as a trade-checking tool, not as an automated trading system. It never accesses your Minecraft account or places a transaction for you.

Before trading

  • Confirm the Buy Instantly total, not only the displayed unit price.
  • Confirm the NPC accepts the item and shows the expected sell value.
  • Prefer a quantity that you can carry and sell without leaving items behind.
